FAQ: How do you enjoy streaming music (Spotify / YouTube Music) via the BenQ GV50 projector?

More tips for you to enjoy streaming music:
If you are looking for more energy-saving music enjoyment with our well-designed speaker, we suggest you enter “Audio Only Mode” which is a customized mode created by BenQ..
1. Open any music streaming APP on the Projector.
2. To enter BenQ's customized  “Audio Only Mode”, please press and hold the "BenQ Projector Assistance" button on the remote control (at the bottom right corner) for two seconds.
3. Adjusting volume through the remote control is available without waking-up the screen.
4. Press any button except the power or volume adjustment buttons on the remote to exit the Audio Only Mode.

FAQ: Can I play media content from a USB flash drive on the projector?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Go to Home > APPs then search for "Media Player" and download your preferred app (VLC is recommended).
2. Connect your USB-C or USB-A flash drives to the projector.
3. Browse your device for the media file to play and then enjoy.

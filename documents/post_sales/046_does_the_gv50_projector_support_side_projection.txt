FAQ: Does the GV50 projector support side projection?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Yes, the GV50 projector supports side projection. It occurs when the projector is not perpendicular to the screen. The screen automatically performs keystone correction as the projector moves.
NOTE: The relevant specifications for keystone correction are:
2D, (Auto keystone correction) Vertical ± 20 degrees, Horizontal ±30 degrees, Rotation ± 30 degrees (distance to the wall by projection direction: within 2 M);
2D, (Manual leystone correction) Vertical and Horizontal and Rotation ± 40 degrees.
If you want to make further adjustments, go to Settings  > Projector > Image Correction > Manual Keystone Correction > and then follow the on-screen instructions to make the adjustments.

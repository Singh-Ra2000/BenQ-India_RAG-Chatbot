FAQ: Can I use the GV50 projector in the daytime?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
The 500 ANSI lumens allows for clear images when you project onto a 80" screen in a dark room, 60" screen in a room with soft lighting, or a 40” screen in a well-lit room with no daylight. When using the projector in the daytime, choose the "Daytime" picture mode to brighten the dim regions of the images.

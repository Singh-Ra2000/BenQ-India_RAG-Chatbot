FAQ: Can I use voice control on the projector through Google Assistant?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Yes. Google Assistant can be used to access apps in areas where it's available. The projector comes with a Bluetooth remote control. Make sure the remote control is connected to the projector via Bluetooth, and then hold the mic button on the remote control to start a voice search.

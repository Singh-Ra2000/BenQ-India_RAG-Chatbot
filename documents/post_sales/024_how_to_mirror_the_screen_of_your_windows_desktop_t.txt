FAQ: How to mirror the screen of your Windows desktop to the BenQ Google TV projector?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Please follow the steps below to mirror the screen of your Windows desktop to the BenQ Google TV projector:
STEP 1
Connect your Windows device to the same Wi-FI network as your Google TV projector
Check the Wi-FI network your Google TV projector is using:
Home screen of Google TV > Settings > Wi-Fi
STEP 2
Open the Google Chrome browser, go to More, and select Cast, save, and share
STEP 3
Click Cast
Click Sources to select Cast Screen
Click BenQGV01 to cast your device screen

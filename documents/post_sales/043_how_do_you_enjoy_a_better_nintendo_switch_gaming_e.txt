FAQ: How do you enjoy a better Nintendo Switch gaming experience with the GV50 Portable Projector?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
The input lag of the GV50 projector is 22.4 ms (1080p@60Hz) under the "Game Boost" picture mode and we suggest placing the projector right in front of the screen since all image corrections will be reset and not adjustable for ultra-low latency.
NOTE:
The picture modes above can only be selected when an HDMI/USB-C source is connected.
To activate the "Game-Boost" or "HDR Game-Boost" setting via the remote control: Go to Settings > Display and Sound > Picture > Picture Mode.
For more details on upgrading the firmware please refer to :

FAQ: How can I stop my projector from showing up as available for pairing with Bluetooth?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Due to the current design limitation with the system, we unfortunately cannot modify the Bluetooth behavior on the projector.
We completely understand that it can be frustrating that the projector cannot turn off Bluetooth pairing broadcast.
To help you avoid any accidental pairing requests from others, we recommend changing the model name of your projector.
To do so, please follow the path:
Settings > All Settings > System > About > Device Name

FAQ: Can I use the GV50 projector in the daytime?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Yes, the projector's 500 ANSI lumen laser light source allows you to use it in the daytime around your house. For a more enjoyable viewing experience, watching in a soft lit or darker environment is suggested.
The 500 ANSI lumen allows for clear images when you project onto an 80" screen in a dark room, a 60" screen in a room with soft lighting, or a 40” screen in a well-lit room with no daylight. When using the projector in the daytime, choose the "Daytime" picture mode to brighten the dim regions of the images.

FAQ: What are the differences between the GV11, GV30, GV31, and GV50 projectors?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
The key feature of the GV series is a free-rotation angle which provides a laid-back movie viewing experience. The differences between the GV11, GV30, GV31, and GV50 lie in their resolutions,  brightness, and the methods of projection angle adjustment. The GV50 is the most up-to-date model with 1080p resolution, 500 ANSI lumens, officially certified Netflix content, and the most convenient way to have comprehensive projection angle adjustments for bedroom cinema enjoyment.

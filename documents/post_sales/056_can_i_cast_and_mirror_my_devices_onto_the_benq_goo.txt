FAQ: Can I cast and mirror my devices onto the BenQ Google TV projector?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
For iOS/macOS devices:
1. Make sure your iOS/macOS devices and the projector are on the same Wi-Fi network.
2. Go to Settings > Projector > Advanced > Screen Sharing for iOS.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions and cast/mirror your iOS device via Airplay.
For Android devices:
1. Make sure your Android devices and the projector are on the same Wi-Fi network.
2. Make sure your projector stays on the Google TV home page.
3. Download the Google Home app from the Google Play Store onto your Android devices.
4. Find the corresponding BenQ Google TV device name (e.g. BenQGV01 for BenQ GV50 Projector) in the Device section of Google Home to begin casting.
*The device name will be change based on your personal adjustments (Home and Room naming settings) during the set-up wizard.

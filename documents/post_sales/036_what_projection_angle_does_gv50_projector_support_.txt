FAQ: What projection angle does GV50 projector support with its uniquely-designed base?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
The projector is designed with a round form factor, making it easy to rotate freely on the magnetic base and even project onto the ceiling.
With its convenient base, the GV50 is able to project in the following scenarios using three main concepts (axes).
360° horizontal spinning.
135° vertical rotation.
Two levels of ceiling side projection tilting stand (10 ° / 15°).
It is strongly suggested that you use the magnetic base for steady rotation.

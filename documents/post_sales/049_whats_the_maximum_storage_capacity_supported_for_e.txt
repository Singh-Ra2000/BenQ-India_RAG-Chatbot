FAQ: What’s the maximum storage capacity supported for external hard drives / external HDD / external SSD?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
You can connect one external hard drive to the projector, with a maximum supported capacity of 2TB. The projector supports commonly used USB external storage devices, including USB external hard drives, external HDDs, portable hard drives, and external SSDs up to 2TB.

FAQ: My Bluetooth headphones can't pair with the projector successfully. What can I do to fix this?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Please follow the below steps.
1. Reboot the projector as well as reset the Bluetooth headphones you're
trying to connect.
2. Let the Bluetooth headphones enter paring mode first.
3. Right after the Bluetooth headphones enter pairing mode, let the QS02
Android TV dongle search for Bluetooth devices.
4. Wait 1 minute while the dongle detects new devices.

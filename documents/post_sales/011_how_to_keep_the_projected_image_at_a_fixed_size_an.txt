FAQ: How to Keep the Projected Image at a Fixed Size and Position?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
To maintain a fixed image size and position, you need to disable the projector’s automatic image adjustment features. Please follow the steps below:
Use the remote control or the control panel on the projector to access the main menu,
go to All settings(The gear icon)
Make sure all the settings in below being set to off:
1. All settings > Projector > Keystone Correction > Keystone Correction Settings >
Auto Keystone Correction At Startup
Auto Keystone Correction After Movement
Avoid Obstacles Always On
2. All settings > Projector > Focus >
Focus Settings
Auto Focus at Startsup
Auto Focus After Movement
Deep Focus Always On

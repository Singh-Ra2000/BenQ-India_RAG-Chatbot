FAQ: How to mirror the screen of your iPhone, iPad, and MacBook to the BenQ Google TV projector?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Please follow the steps below to mirror the screen of your iPhone, iPad, and MacBook to the BenQ Google TV projector:
STEP 1
Go to Settings
Click All settings
STEP 2
Go to Projector
Select Advanced Settings
STEP 3
Click Screen Sharing for iOS
You will be directed to Play Store as the following picture if Screen sharing for iOS is not installed on your Google TV. Click Install to install Screen sharing for iOS
STEP 4
Click Open to run Screen sharing for iOS
Click Agree
STEP 5
Select Go to Settings
Scroll down to Screen sharing for iOS
STEP 6
Click OK on the remote to enable Screen sharing for iOS, then press Back on the remote
Select While using the app
STEP 7
Go to Control Center on your iPhone, iPad, or MacBook, and tap/select screen mirroring. Select BenQGV01 to mirror the screen of your iPhone, iPad, or MacBook
You would see the following picture once the screen of your iPhone, iPad, or MacBook is successfully mirrored to your Google TV projector.

FAQ: How do you connect your external Bluetooth devices with the Google TV? Will there be any audio lag?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Connect your Bluetooth devices to Google TV by following these steps:
1. Activate the Bluetooth-pairing mode of your external Bluetooth devices.
2. Go to Settings > Remotes and Accessories > Pair Accessories to add
accessories to connect the Bluetooth device.
NOTE:
If you want to connect to external Bluetooth speakers or headphones, there will be a minor audio lag of around 300 ms. It is okay for listening to music but you may experience minor latency while watching movies.
You can only connect ONE audio Bluetooth device at a time. If you are using different Bluetooth devices, please connect again.

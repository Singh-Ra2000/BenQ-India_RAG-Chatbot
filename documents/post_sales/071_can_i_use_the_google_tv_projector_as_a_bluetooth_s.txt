FAQ: Can I use the Google TV projector as a Bluetooth speaker?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
When you press the “Bluetooth Speaker” hotkey on the top of the projector, the projector will stop projecting and switch to “Bluetooth Speaker Mode”, which allows you to use it as a Bluetooth speaker.
1. Go to Settings > Projector > Bluetooth Speaker Mode.
2. Find the device "BenQGV01" on your phone and pair with it.
* The device name will be changed based on your personal adjustments (Home & Room naming settings) during the set-up wizard.
3. To exit the Bluetooth Speaker Mode, simply press “Back” for two seconds.

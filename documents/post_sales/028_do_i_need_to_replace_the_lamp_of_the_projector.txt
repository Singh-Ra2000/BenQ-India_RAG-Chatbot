FAQ: Do I need to replace the lamp of the projector?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
There is no need to replace it during the normal usage lifetime of the projector.
Because the projector uses a laser light source instead of a lamp, it can last for about 20,000 to 30,000 hours, so there is no need to change it during the normal usage lifetime of the projector. If you want to change the lamp, please notice that laser replacement is not user-serviceable, please contact a service center to replace it.

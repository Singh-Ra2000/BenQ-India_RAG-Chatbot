FAQ: How does keystone correction work on the GV50 projector?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Keystone correction can be set up to work instantly with the projector's built-in autofocus and auto 2D keystone functions.
NOTE: The relevant specifications for keystone correction are:
2D, (Auto keystone correction) Vertical ± 20 degrees, Horizontal ±30 degrees, Rotation ± 30 degrees (distance to the wall by projection direction: within 2 M)
2D, (Manual keystone correction) Vertical and Horizontal and Rotation ± 40 degrees.

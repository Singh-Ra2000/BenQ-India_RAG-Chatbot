FAQ: What type of connections support HDR10/HLG on the GV50 projector?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
The projector will automatically detect HDR10/HLG and display compatible signals from sources connected to the HDMI ports. Furthermore, files from the USB-A 3.0 port (viewed on the Media Reader) also support HDR10.

FAQ: Can I cast and mirror my devices onto the BenQ Google TV projector?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Yes, you can cast and mirror your devices onto the BenQ Smart Home projector, please follow the instructions below:
For iOS/macOS  devices:
1. Make sure your iOS/macOS devices and the projector are on the same Wi-Fi network.
2. Go to Home > APPS > Screen Sharing for iOS or Go to Settings > Projector > Advanced >Screen Sharing for iOS.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions and cast/mirror your iOS device via Airplay
For Android devices:
1. Make sure your Android devices and the projector are on the same Wi-Fi network.
2. Make sure your projector stays on the Google TV home page.
3. Download the Google Home app from the Google Play Store on your Android devices.
4. Find the corresponding BenQ Google TV device name (x: BenQXX for BenQ XX Projector) in the Device section of Google Home to begin casting.
*Device name will be changed based on your personal adjustment(Home & Room naming settings ) during the set-up wizard.

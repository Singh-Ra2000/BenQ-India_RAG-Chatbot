FAQ: Can I play media content from a USB flash drive on the projector?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Yes, you can do so. Please refer to the following steps:
Go to Home > APPs then search for "Media Player" and download your preferred app (VLC is recommended).
Connect your USB-C or USB-A flash drives to the projector.
Browse your device for the media file to play and then enjoy.
